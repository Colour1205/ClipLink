Features

On your local network

Devices on the same Wi-Fi find each other and connect directly.

Files up to 1 GB

Text, pictures and real files, not links.

The last 25 items

Kept on every device. Delete one and it's gone everywhere.

Pair once with a QR code

Scan the code on your other device. After that they find each other on their own.

Encrypted between your devices

Only devices you paired can read what you copy. No account and no server.

Away from home too

Pair by address over a VPN such as Tailscale.

Privacy

Everything between your devices is encrypted, and every item is signed by the device it came from. How it works

A device can't read or send anything until you pair with it and choose Trust.

FAQ

Does my clipboard go through a server?

No. Items go straight between your paired devices. The only outside request any ClipLink app makes is the Windows update check to GitHub, described in the privacy policy.

Can someone else on my Wi-Fi see what I copy?

No. They can see that a ClipLink device is there, but never what you copy. Items only go to devices you trust, encrypted.

Do my devices have to be on the same Wi-Fi?

To find each other on their own, yes. Otherwise you can pair by address over a VPN such as Tailscale.

Why does my phone only sync when the app is open?

iOS and HarmonyOS don't let apps run in the background, so they catch up when you open ClipLink. Android can keep receiving in the background if you turn it on in the Me tab. Windows syncs all the time from the tray.

What does ClipLink keep on my device?

The last 25 items and their files, in the app's own storage. You can delete single items or clear the whole history.

How do I update ClipLink?

On Windows, open Settings, then About, and click Download and install. Phone apps update through their store, or you install a newer APK over the old one.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

The beta installer isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. See the code signing policy.

Is it free?

Yes. It's open source under Apache 2.0, and the code is on GitHub.
