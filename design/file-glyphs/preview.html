ClipLink file glyphs

Fifteen file-type glyphs in the type-chip style: 24×24, white 2 px round strokes, drawn at 52% of an accent tile with a corner radius of size/3.2. Nothing here is in the apps yet.

1. Glyphs at 36 px and 18 px

Left tile is the grid card size, right tile is the subtitle-row size.
